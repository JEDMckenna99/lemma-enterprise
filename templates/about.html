Building the Verified Internet

Lemma exists to solve one fundamental problem: proving that a user is a unique entity (human or authorized agent), nothing more and nothing less.

Our Mission

To become the essential verification standard for digital platforms, starting with individual site protection and growing toward broader network effects as adoption scales. We're building tomorrow's trust infrastructure.

What We Believe

Privacy is Paramount

We collect only what's necessary to verify user authenticity, protecting user data while enabling trust.

Simplicity is Powerful

A focused solution that does one thing exceptionally well creates more value than complex systems.

Offline Verification Matters

By enabling credential verification without requiring an active internet connection, we create a more resilient digital ecosystem.

Self-Sovereignty is Essential

Users should control their own identity and determine what information they share with whom.

The Problem We're Solving

The Bot Crisis

Nearly 40% of internet traffic is non-human. With AI making bot detection increasingly impossible, businesses need a reliable way to ensure they're interacting with legitimate users.

Trust Infrastructure Gap

The internet lacks a foundational verification layer. Every platform builds their own solution, creating friction for users and inefficiency for businesses.
