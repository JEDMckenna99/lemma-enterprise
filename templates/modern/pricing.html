Frequently Asked Questions

How does active user billing work?

You're billed $0.10 for each unique user who visits your site and gets verified through Lemma Shield each month. Only users who are actually active (visiting your site) in a given month are counted for billing.

What's included in the price?

Everything: microsecond verification, bot detection, privacy protection, analytics dashboard, API access, and 24/7 support. No hidden fees or feature restrictions.

When is the $2 Stripe Identity fee charged?

The $2.00 fee is only charged when a user goes through initial Stripe Identity verification (KYC process). Not every user requires this - only those who need enhanced identity verification. Regular users who just visit your site are only charged the $0.10 MAU fee.

How fast is verification?

Lemma Shield verifies users in microseconds (4.176µs average) - that's 1000x faster than traditional solutions. 99.9% of verifications work offline.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! Cancel anytime with no penalties. You'll only be charged for users verified in the current billing cycle. No long-term contracts or commitments.

How does this compare to competitors?

Lemma Shield costs 90% less than alternatives like Auth0 ($23/month per 1000 users) or Okta ($2/user/month), while providing faster, more secure verification.
