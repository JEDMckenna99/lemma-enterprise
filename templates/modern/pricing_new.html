How billing works

Pricing has two parts: identity verification (for isHuman assurance), passed through at cost, and the lemma.id proof binding — the reusable site-private credential and continuity infrastructure we build on top. You pay for the binding once, plus a small monthly fee only when a user returns. Everything else is free.

Identity verification

$0.33 / user · at cost

A one-time isHuman check (document + liveness) the first time a person needs proof-of-human uniqueness on your site. Passed through at cost — lemma.id adds no markup. Only required when your policy needs Sybil resistance; not repeated for routine continuity.

lemma.id proof binding

$0.50 / user · one-time

What lemma.id adds on top: a site-private, reusable proof credential your users hold in their own wallet. The same wallet returns with the same PPID — no re-verification, no identity data on your servers. This is the lemma.id platform fee.

Monthly active user (MAU)

$0.03 / user / month

Charged for each calendar month a returning user renews their credential, starting the month after binding. Covers continuity, revocation, and keeping zero identity data on your servers. Inactive months are not billed.

Local verification

Free

Your servers validate credentials locally with the SDK — signature, expiry, and revocation. Silent, no network call, unlimited, never metered. Credentials expire after 30 days; renewal opens a lemma.id popup.

Billing rules

  1. Identity verification ($0.33): billed once when a person completes isHuman for your site. Passed through at cost; never marked up and not repeated for routine continuity.
  2. Proof binding ($0.50): the one-time lemma.id platform fee for issuing the user's first site-private, reusable credential and wallet binding (passkey secures the wallet on first use).
  3. MAU ($0.03): billed for each calendar month a user renews after the month of their binding. The binding month itself has no MAU charge.
  4. Active user: a user whose credential is re-issued that month — typically when their prior credential expires (~30 days) and they complete the continuity popup. Daily local checks never count.
  5. Same wallet, same PPID: renewals issue a fresh credential with the same site-private PPID — no repeat verification for routine continuity. With isHuman, the person root keeps that PPID stable even across assurance step-ups.
  6. Local checks: unlimited presentations and verifications on your infrastructure between renewals — never metered.
  7. Per site: each relying site is billed independently for its own users (pairwise, non-correlatable PPIDs).

Example: 1,000 new users in January, 600 return in February

January: 1,000 × ($0.33 verification + $0.50 binding) = $830. No MAU charges — the binding month is excluded.

February: 600 returning active users × $0.03 = $18.00 MAU. Plus any new users who join in February at $0.83 each.

All year: A user who visits daily but renews once when their credential expires counts as one MAU that month — not one charge per login or local verification.
