How It Works

Your identity lives in your wallet, syncs across devices, and gives each site only what they need to know - nothing more.

1

Create Once

Set up your wallet with a passkey. No password needed - your device is the key.

2

Sync Everywhere

Link any device. Your wallet syncs automatically - phone, laptop, tablet.

3

Sign In Anywhere

One tap on any network site. Each sees a unique pseudonym - unlinkable.

Privacy-Preserving Login

Each site sees a unique identifier for you (PPID). Sites cannot track you across the network or share your data with each other.

Cross-Device Sync

Wallet data encrypted end-to-end and synced across all your devices. Lost your phone? Sign in on any linked device.

Passkey Security

Biometric or device-based authentication. No passwords to remember, phish, or breach. Hardware-backed security.

Network Effect

One wallet works across all sites on the Lemma network. More sites join, more places you can sign in - no new accounts needed.

Permission Control

Grant and revoke access per site. See exactly what each site can access. You're in control, not them.

Instant Verification

Credentials verified locally in your browser using Ed25519 signatures. No server round-trips for authentication.
