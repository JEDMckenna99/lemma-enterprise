A "lemma" is a cryptographic permission credential that proves access rights without revealing sensitive data. Think of it as a digital key that can't be forged, copied, or tampered with.

Users receive permission lemmas via email confirmation. These credentials are stored in their browser wallet (not your database), verified in microseconds (182µs), and work offline. Using Ed25519 signatures and zero-knowledge proofs, lemmas provide enterprise-grade security without the complexity or cost of traditional IAM systems.
