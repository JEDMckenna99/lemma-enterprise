What is Lemma?

A next-generation verification engine inspired by mathematical lemmas—compact, proven statements that serve as building blocks for larger proofs.

In the digital world, a lemma is a privacy-preserving cryptographic proof (e.g., "this user is a verified human" or "this device is authentic") that can be generated once and verified instantly anywhere in the network.

Using advanced techniques like zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs), lemmas allow selective disclosure—proving specific claims (e.g., "over 18") without revealing underlying data. This creates better authenticated networks where communication is secure, unlinkable, and resistant to bots or fraud, all with >99.9% offline operation and microsecond-level performance (proven at 4.176µs in production).
