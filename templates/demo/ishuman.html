Why Lemma

Problem framing for buyers

From IP bans to human accountability

Today, bot defense usually punishes infrastructure. Lemma lets a business respond at the human-proof layer.

Current bot defense

  • Detect bot-like behavior.
  • Ban IPs, ISPs, VPNs, devices, or regions.
  • Real users get caught in the blast radius.
  • Attackers rotate infrastructure and come back.

With Lemma

  • Detect bot-like behavior.
  • Block the site-private human proof.
  • Require a fresh identity check to continue.
  • Escalate confirmed abuse to network revocation.

Why businesses care

Without Lemma

  • CAPTCHA slows real users and is cheap for attackers.
  • IP and ISP bans create collateral damage.
  • Full identity verification is too expensive for most signups and trials.
  • Attackers rotate proxies and accounts.

With Lemma

  • Users reuse one human proof across sites.
  • Businesses block private human IDs, not whole networks.
  • Suspicious users can be forced through fresh verification.
  • Confirmed abuse can revoke the reusable proof everywhere.
