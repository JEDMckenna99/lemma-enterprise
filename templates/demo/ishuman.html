Step 3 — Use demo sites

Reserve a ticket or start a trial — the site stamps your action with a signed presentation and verifies it on its own server, with no call back to lemma.id.

Why this step

The integrator path: the browser SDK mints the stamp, your backend verifies it offline. No per-request call to lemma.id keeps checks fast and private.

Step 4 — Escalate to isHuman (same PPID)

On doubt or a high-risk action, ticketing raises its bar to isHuman and demands a fresh verification. Complete IDV, then re-verify — the PPID is unchanged; only the assurance flips from passkey to ishuman.

Why this step

Step-up strengthens the same account, not a new one: the site-private ID is stable across tiers, so a fresh isHuman proof raises trust without re-registering the user.
