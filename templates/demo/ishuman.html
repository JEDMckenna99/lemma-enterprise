isHuman Proof Network

Bot defense should punish abusive humans, not whole networks.

Lemma gives businesses a reusable human proof and a way to reimpose real IDV pressure when behavior looks non-human, instead of banning IPs, ISPs, VPNs, or broad device fingerprints.

What Lemma does

It turns one trusted identity verification into a reusable, locally checkable human proof. Businesses can allow real users, block abusive site-private IDs, and force a fresh IDV challenge when behavior stops looking human.

1

User verifies once

The user completes an IDV-backed check and stores a signed isHuman proof in their browser wallet.

2

Business checks at action time

A customer site calls the hosted Lemma verifier when the user reserves tickets or starts a trial.

3

Abuse triggers accountability

The site can block the private ID, request a fresh IDV challenge, or escalate confirmed abuse for network revocation.

From IP bans to human accountability

Today, bot defense usually punishes infrastructure. Lemma lets a business respond at the human-proof layer.

Current bot defense

  • Detect bot-like behavior.
  • Ban IPs, ISPs, VPNs, devices, or regions.
  • Real users get caught in the blast radius.
  • Attackers rotate infrastructure and come back.

With Lemma

  • Detect bot-like behavior.
  • Block the site-private human proof.
  • Require a fresh IDV challenge to continue.
  • Escalate confirmed abuse to network revocation.

Why businesses care

Without Lemma

  • CAPTCHA slows real users and is cheap for attackers.

  • Full IDV is too expensive for most signups and trials.
  • Attackers rotate proxies and accounts.

With Lemma
