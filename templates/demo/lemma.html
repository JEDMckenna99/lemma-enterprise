Why integrators use lemma.id

Without lemma.id

  • Each site runs its own verification — users repeat signup friction everywhere
  • You store emails, phones, or device signals to recognize the same person
  • Network-wide blocklists — one ban follows users across unrelated sites

With lemma.id

  • One lemma.id gives users continuity without repeating signup friction everywhere
  • Sites request site-private proofs from that lemma.id; your backend verifies them offline
  • Require human proofs for higher-risk flows where one account must map to one verified human

3. Match assurance to your risk

A blocked user can mint a new lemma.id — new passkey, new private ID. Whether that matters depends on your flow: a comment section may accept it; a ticket drop won't. Sites that care raise the bar to human proofs.

A policy decision, not a flaw

Passkey assurance gives continuity and privacy with zero signup friction — the right default for most flows. Sybil resistance is a separate dial: sites turn it on by requiring human proofs where one account must map to one human.
