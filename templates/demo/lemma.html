Why integrators use lemma.id

Without lemma.id

  • Each site runs its own verification — users repeat signup friction everywhere
  • You store emails, phones, or device signals to recognize the same person
  • Network-wide blocklists — one ban follows users across unrelated sites

With lemma.id

  • One passkey wallet gives users continuity without repeating signup friction everywhere
  • Sites request site-private stamps from that wallet; your backend verifies them offline
  • Require human proofs for higher-risk flows where one account must map to one verified human

4. Watch the ban break

An abuser mints a fresh empty lemma.id — new passkey, new site-private ID. Your passkey-only block no longer matches.

Why this step

Empty wallets are free to create. Passkey assurance gives continuity and privacy, but not Sybil resistance — a determined abuser rotates wallets faster than you can block individual PPIDs.
