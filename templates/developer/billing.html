How you're billed

  • $0.50 — first site credential per user, one-time (identity verification included when human proofs are required)
  • $0.03 — monthly active user (renewal starting the month after binding)
  • $0.33 — doubt re-entry after an active site doubt (fresh IDV, passed through at cost)
  • $0 — site blocks, hard bans, local verification

Invoices are generated monthly from metered usage. Failed payments pause new credential issuance until resolved.
