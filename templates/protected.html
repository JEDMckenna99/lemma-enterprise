Lemma Network Pricing Model

                
                
Lemma's innovative pricing approach is designed to create a network effect - the more organizations that join the Lemma network, the more affordable it becomes for everyone.

                
                
One-Time Onboarding Fee

                
A modest one-time verification fee ($1-$2 per user) covers the cost of identity verification through Stripe Identity. This fee is charged only once per unique human in the network, regardless of how many sites they access with their Lemma credential.

Advantages Over Traditional Pricing

                
Unlike traditional identity and security services that charge a fixed per-user rate regardless of network size, Lemma's network-effect pricing creates shared value:

                
    
                    
  • Early adopters benefit as the network grows
    • 
                    
  • The cost of maintaining unique human verification is distributed across all participants
    • 
                    
  • As adoption increases, costs decrease while the value of the network increases
    • 
                    
  • Pricing aligns with the value received - you only pay for active users who benefit from Lemma verification
    • 
                

                
                
This pricing approach reflects Lemma's fundamental belief that maintaining a network of verified humans should become more affordable as it becomes more universal.
