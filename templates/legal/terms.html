2. Description of Service

Lemma.id provides proof continuity for web applications. The Service includes:

  • lemma.id: a passkey-protected local identity store the user holds. Credentials are stored on the user's device; we issue and sign them.
  • Site-private PPIDs: a stable opaque identifier for one person on one canonical hostname. Other sites receive different PPIDs they cannot join to each other.
  • Local Return-Visit Verification: For routine sign-in checks, your servers can verify credentials locally using Ed25519 signatures without per-request calls to Lemma (initial IDV and optional server verification paths do contact Lemma).
  • Optional human proofs (isHuman): IDV-backed person assurance on the same PPID, rooted in the verified document. This is not biometric unique-human identification. Distinct documents on a fresh lemma.id can still mint distinct persons.
  • Revocation and enforcement: Bloom-filter revocation snapshots, plus site-block and site-doubt APIs the relying site controls for its own PPIDs
  • Developer SDK: browser and server verifiers for signed presentations
  • Usage metering: billing for human-proof binding, monthly renewals, and doubt re-entry as described on the Pricing page

The Service is a proof layer. Unless you choose to replace your login stack, it does not become your sole identity provider, and a valid proof does not grant site administration unless a separate permission proof says so.
