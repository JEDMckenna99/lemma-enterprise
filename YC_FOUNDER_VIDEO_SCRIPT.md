## ⚠️ **WHAT NOT TO SAY:**

### **Avoid:**
- ❌ "We're like Auth0 but..." (you're NOT like them, you're different)
- ❌ "We might pivot to..." (shows lack of conviction)
- ❌ "We're still figuring out..." (shows lack of clarity)
- ❌ Technical jargon without context ("OPRF", "W3C DIDs")
- ❌ Weak claims ("We think we can...", "We hope to...")

### **Instead:**
- ✅ "Auth0 does X, we do Y" (clear contrast)
- ✅ "We're building Z" (definitive)
- ✅ "We've built X" (concrete achievements)
- ✅ "This works because..." (clear explanation)
- ✅ "We will..." (confident vision)
