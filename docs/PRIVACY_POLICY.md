## 🎯 **OUR PRIVACY PRINCIPLES**

### **Privacy by Design**
- **Data Minimization:** We collect only the minimum data necessary for human verification
- **Purpose Limitation:** Data is used solely for verification and fraud prevention
- **Storage Minimization:** No central database of verified users - credentials stored client-side
- **Transparency:** Clear information about all data processing activities

### **User Control**
- **Self-Sovereign Identity:** You control your verification credentials
- **Portable Credentials:** Your verification works across all Lemma network sites
- **Right to Deletion:** Complete data deletion upon request
- **Consent Management:** Clear opt-in for all data processing

## 📊 **INFORMATION WE COLLECT**

### **1. Verification Data (Temporary)**

**During Identity Verification:**
- **Identity Documents:** Processed temporarily through Stripe Identity (deleted immediately after verification)
- **Biometric Data:** Facial recognition for liveness detection (not stored by Lemma)
- **Verification Results:** Only the fact that you are human (no personal details stored)

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interests (fraud prevention) and contract performance  
**Retention:** Deleted immediately after verification completion

### **2. Usage Analytics (Pseudonymized)**

**For Service Improvement:**
- **Verification Events:** Timestamps and success/failure status
- **Site Integration:** Which sites use our verification (no user linkage)
- **Performance Metrics:** Response times and error rates

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interests (service improvement)  
**Retention:** 31 days for raw data, aggregated data retained indefinitely

### **3. Customer Account Data**

**For Business Customers:**
- **Contact Information:** Email, company name, billing address
- **API Usage:** Request volumes and billing calculations
- **Support Communications:** Help desk tickets and responses

**Legal Basis:** Contract performance and legal obligations  
**Retention:** 7 years for billing data, 3 years for support data

### **4. Technical Data**

**For Security and Performance:**
- **IP Addresses:** For rate limiting and fraud detection (hashed after 24 hours)
- **Session Tokens:** Temporary authentication (deleted after session)
- **Error Logs:** System errors and debugging information (no personal data)

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interests (security and performance)  
**Retention:** 31 days for raw logs, aggregated metrics retained indefinitely

### **Primary Purposes**

1. **Human Verification**

3. **Service Delivery**
   - Process verification requests and responses
   - Maintain service availability and performance
   - Provide customer support and technical assistance

### **Secondary Purposes**

4. **Service Improvement**
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve performance
   - Develop new features and capabilities
   - Optimize user experience and integration

5. **Business Operations**
   - Calculate billing and usage metrics
   - Generate invoices and process payments
   - Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
