## 🎯 **CLAUSE 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

### **1.1 Purpose**
These Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) set out the rights and obligations of the data exporter and the data importer when transferring personal data from the European Union to third countries that do not ensure an adequate level of protection.

### **1.2 Scope**
These SCCs apply to the transfer of personal data as specified in Annex I.B for the purposes of providing human verification services as described in the Master Service Agreement.

### **1.3 Data Protection Laws**
These SCCs are without prejudice to obligations to which the data exporter is subject by virtue of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

---

## 🛡️ **CLAUSE 2: EFFECT AND INVARIABILITY OF THE CLAUSES**

### **2.1 Binding Effect**
These SCCs set out appropriate safeguards pursuant to Articles 46(1) and 46(2)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 for the transfer of personal data to a third country.

### **2.2 Invariability**
The parties undertake not to modify these SCCs, except for adding business-related information to the Annexes or additional safeguards provided they do not contradict these SCCs.

---

## 📋 **CLAUSE 3: THIRD-PARTY BENEFICIARIES**

### **3.1 Data Subjects as Beneficiaries**
Data subjects may invoke and enforce these SCCs as third-party beneficiaries against the data exporter and/or data importer.

### **3.2 Compensation**
Data subjects are entitled to receive compensation from the data exporter or data importer for any material or non-material damage resulting from a breach of these SCCs.

---

## 🔒 **CLAUSE 4: INTERPRETATION**

### **4.1 Definitions**
Terms used in these SCCs have the same meaning as in Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

### **4.2 Precedence**
In case of inconsistency between these SCCs and the provisions of related agreements, these SCCs shall prevail.

---

## ⚖️ **CLAUSE 5: HIERARCHY**

In case of inconsistency between these SCCs and the provisions of related agreements between the parties existing at the time when these SCCs are agreed or entered into thereafter, these SCCs shall prevail.

---

## 📋 **CLAUSE 6: DESCRIPTION OF TRANSFERS**

The details of the transfer(s) and in particular the categories of personal data that are transferred and the purpose(s) for which they are transferred are specified in Annex I.B.

---

## 🛡️ **CLAUSE 7: DOCKING CLAUSE**

An entity that is not a party to these SCCs may, with the agreement of the parties, accede to these SCCs at any time as a data exporter or data importer by completing the Annexes and signing Annex I.A.

---

## 🔒 **CLAUSE 8: DATA PROTECTION SAFEGUARDS**

### **8.1 Processing Instructions**
The data importer shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the data exporter, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organisation.

### **8.2 Purpose Limitation**
The data importer shall inform the data exporter immediately if it is unable to follow those instructions, including if such instructions infringe applicable local law in the country of destination.

### **8.3 Confidentiality**
The data importer shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

### **8.4 Security Measures**
The data importer shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
- Encryption of personal data
- Ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of processing systems
- Regular testing, assessing and evaluating the effectiveness of measures

### **8.5 Data Subject Rights**
The data importer shall assist the data exporter in responding to data subject requests and shall:
- Inform data subjects of their rights under these SCCs
- Accept and process requests from data subjects
- Forward requests to the data exporter when unable to respond directly

---

## 📞 **CLAUSE 9: USE OF SUB-PROCESSORS**

### **9.1 Prior Authorization**
The data importer shall not sub-contract any of its processing activities performed on behalf of the data exporter under these SCCs to a sub-processor without the prior specific written authorisation of the data exporter.

### **9.2 Sub-processor Obligations**
Where the data importer engages a sub-processor for carrying out specific processing activities, it shall do so by way of a written contract that provides for the same data protection obligations as those binding the data importer under these SCCs.

### **9.3 Liability**
The data importer shall remain fully liable to the data exporter for the performance of the sub-processor's obligations under its contract with the data importer.

---

## 🚨 **CLAUSE 10: DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS**

### **10.1 Transparent Information**
The data importer shall inform data subjects in a transparent and easily accessible format about its role and the processing of their personal data.

### **10.2 Rights Exercise**
Data subjects may exercise their rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 in relation to the processing of their personal data by the data importer.

---

## 🔍 **CLAUSE 11: REDRESS**

### **11.1 Complaint Mechanism**
Data subjects shall have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority and to an effective judicial remedy.

### **11.2 Compensation**
Data subjects shall be entitled to receive compensation for any material or non-material damage resulting from a breach of these SCCs.

---

## 📋 **CLAUSE 12: LIABILITY**

### **12.1 Joint Liability**
Each party shall be liable to the other party for any damages it causes by any breach of these SCCs.

### **12.2 Data Subject Compensation**
The parties agree that if one party is held liable for a violation of these SCCs by the other party, the latter will, to the extent liable, indemnify the first party for any cost, charge, damages, expenses or loss it has incurred.

---

## ⚖️ **CLAUSE 13: SUPERVISION**

### **13.1 Supervisory Authority**
The supervisory authority with responsibility for ensuring compliance by the data exporter with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 shall act as competent supervisory authority.

### **13.2 Cooperation**
The data importer agrees to submit to the jurisdiction of and cooperate with the competent supervisory authority in any procedures aimed at ensuring compliance with these SCCs.

---

## 🔄 **CLAUSE 14: LOCAL LAWS AND OBLIGATIONS**

### **14.1 Disclosure Requirements**
The data importer warrants that it has no reason to believe that the laws and practices in the third country of destination prevent it from fulfilling the obligations under these SCCs.

### **14.2 Government Access**
The data importer shall inform the data exporter if it becomes aware that it is or has become subject to laws or practices that may prevent compliance with these SCCs.

### **14.3 Transparency**
The data importer shall document and upon request make available to the data exporter any requests for disclosure of personal data by public authorities.

---

## 📋 **CLAUSE 15: OBLIGATIONS IN CASE OF ACCESS BY PUBLIC AUTHORITIES**

### **15.1 Notification**
The data importer shall notify the data exporter and, where possible, the data subject promptly of any legally binding request for disclosure of personal data by a public authority.

### **15.2 Challenge**
The data importer shall challenge the request if it has reasonable grounds to consider that the request is unlawful under the laws of the country of origin.

### **15.3 Documentation**
The data importer shall document its assessment and any challenge to the request and make this information available to the data exporter.

---

## 🔄 **CLAUSE 16: NON-COMPLIANCE AND TERMINATION**

### **16.1 Suspension**
The data exporter shall suspend the transfer of personal data to the data importer if it becomes aware that the data importer is in breach of its obligations under these SCCs.

### **16.2 Termination**
Either party may terminate these SCCs, in relation to some or all categories of personal data, by giving notice to the other party.

### **16.3 Data Return**
Upon termination of these SCCs, the data importer shall, at the choice of the data exporter, return all personal data transferred under these SCCs and delete existing copies.

---

## ⚖️ **CLAUSE 17: GOVERNING LAW**

These SCCs shall be governed by the law of the EU Member State in which the data exporter is established.

---

## 📋 **CLAUSE 18: CHOICE OF FORUM AND JURISDICTION**

Any dispute arising from these SCCs shall be resolved by the courts of an EU Member State.
