## 📋 **Ticketing System**

### **Support Portal Features**
- **Ticket Creation:** Web-based ticket submission with attachments
- **Priority Selection:** Auto-routing based on issue classification
- **Real-time Updates:** Email and Slack notifications for ticket progress
- **Knowledge Base:** Searchable documentation and common solutions
- **Escalation Tracking:** Automatic escalation when SLA thresholds are reached

### **Ticket Lifecycle**
1. **Created:** Ticket submitted via portal, email, or phone
2. **Acknowledged:** Auto-response confirming receipt within SLA
3. **Assigned:** Routed to appropriate support engineer
4. **In Progress:** Engineer working on resolution
5. **Pending Customer:** Waiting for customer information
6. **Resolved:** Solution provided, awaiting customer confirmation
7. **Closed:** Customer confirms resolution or auto-closes after 3 days
