```
[JED]: Current auth systems like Auth0 are expensive because they

[JAKE]: This creates a fundamentally different cost structure. 
We can undercut every competitor while maintaining better performance.

[JED]: I've built the complete system - email login, permission 
management, cryptographic revocation. The beta is ready to launch.

[JAKE]: The auth market is $20 billion, and we're positioned to 
take share from Auth0, Clerk, and Okta.

[JED]: We're looking for YC's help to scale this to enterprises.

[JAKE]: Thanks for watching.
```

```
build this into the authentication standard. Thanks for watching.
```
